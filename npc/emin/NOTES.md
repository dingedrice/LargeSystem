The two `analysis.ipynb` files shows the how the structure of the Nuclear Pore Complex (NPC) was treated before and after energy minimization to obtain a symmetrized octamer with released energy.

The two `emin_steps.py` files run the energy minimization with OpenMM on the Aries cluster.

The resulting structures, `constricted_reconstruct.pdb` and `dilated_reconstruct.pdb`, were used to generate the SBM for the simulations.
